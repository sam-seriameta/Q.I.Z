# Instructions

A step-by-step guide. You need no more computer skill than this: open a file,
type in a box, drag a window.

---

## 1. Get the program

Open the app page, pick your language at the top right, then press **Save**,
the pink button at the top. You end up with a file called `regia-quiz.html`.
Put it somewhere you will find it again: the Desktop is fine.

That file **is** the program. Nothing to install, no internet needed. Copy it
to a USB stick and take it anywhere.

> **Your browser may warn you** that the file came from the internet. That
> happens with any downloaded file: choose to open it anyway.

## 2. Open it

Double-click the file. It opens in your browser and you see:

- at the **top**, a bar with **Save**, the **More** menu and the language
- on the **left**, *Setup*: where you prepare everything, split into four tabs
  (**Games**, **Teams**, **Appearance**, **Keys**)
- on the **right**, *Live*: where you run the show and see a preview of what
  the audience sees

Nobody in the audience will ever see this page.

## 3. Connect the projector

Connect the projector or second screen **before** you start. Then, at the top
of the right column, press **Open external screen**: a second window appears.

Drag it onto the second screen, click inside it and press **F** on the
keyboard: it goes fullscreen. That is the window the audience sees.

> If nothing happens, the browser blocked the window. A message appears at the
> top: allow pop-ups for this page and try again.

## 4. Add the teams

Open the **Teams** tab. For each one type a name and pick a colour with the
little square, or one of the ready-made dots.

The colour tells the audience whose turn it is: it tints the waves, the name
tag and the time bar.

**Add team** gives you more. You write teams once and then reuse them in every
round.

## 5. Build the games

A **game** is one challenge (say "Guess the word"). Inside it sit the
**rounds**, usually one per team.

Open the **Games** tab, open the game and name it. Then pick the **template**,
which decides what shows on the big screen:

| Template | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Timer only** | Just the countdown, large and centred |
| **Timer and words** | The clock starts and the first word appears; you move on with the arrow keys |
| **Words and answers** | Same, but after each question you can reveal the answer |

Inside each round pick the team, the length, and type the items:
**one per line**.

If the round does not belong to any team (a question for everyone, say), pick
**No team**: the screen keeps the theme colours and shows no name.

For the answers template, write question and answer on the same line,
separated by a vertical bar `|`:

```
Capital of Sardinia | Cagliari
The island's longest river | Tirso
```

You can write the length in seconds (`90`) or in minutes and seconds (`1:30`).
Any number works.

The **↑ ↓** arrows reorder the rounds. **Duplicate** makes a copy, handy for
building the next team's round.

Each game can also have its own end-of-round behaviour and sound.

## 6. Change the look

Open the **Appearance** tab. Everything you touch shows immediately in the
preview on the right.

**Ready-made themes.** At the top there are five styles (SAM, Night, Minimal,
High contrast, Paper). One click changes colours and decorations together;
then you can fine-tune everything by hand. Your typeface and background image
stay as they are.

Below, in order:

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Colours** | Background, text, the timer colours when time is running out. Untick *Answer in the team colour* to pick a fixed colour for answers |
| **Decoration** | The coloured waves top and bottom, the dots in the corners, the time bar |
| **Background image** | A photo or texture behind every slide. The *veil* slider lightens it so words stay readable |
| **Typeface** | Load your own font and set its size, weight, spacing, italics, all caps |
| **Transition between words** | How each word comes in: none, fade, slide up or pop |
| **Holding card** | The screen the audience sees between rounds: your logo and a line like "Back shortly" |
| **Corner logo** | A small logo on the slides, in one of the four corners |
| **Team name** | Where the name shows before the round starts: left, centre or right |
| **Timer** | Position and size of the timer, and after how many seconds it changes colour |
| **End of round** | What the screen does when time runs out, the sound and the volume |
| **Your sounds** | Upload your own sounds (mp3, wav, ogg…) and pick them as the end sound |

The built-in sounds are: Bell, Beep, Buzzer, Buzzer TV, Ding ding, Countdown,
Gong, Arcade, Sax, Steel drum, Pizzicato, Dramatic hit. Picking one from the
menu plays it straight away. Keep your own sounds short: they end up inside
the saved file and make it heavier.

> If background and text are too close in tone we warn you: from the back of
> the room it would be unreadable.

### Going back

When you change a setting, a small **↺** arrow appears next to it. Click it
and that one setting goes back to how it was at the **last save**. If you have
never saved, it goes back to how it started.

If you changed a lot (trying a ready-made theme, say), **Back to the last
save** appears at the top of the panel: it restores the whole look at once.

### A different look for each game

With **at least two games**, **Theme for** appears at the top of Appearance.
Pick a game and press **Create a theme for this game**: it starts as a copy of
the main theme and you can change it without touching the other games. **Copy
the theme from another game** reuses one you have already set up.

You can also get there from the Games tab, with the **Edit appearance** button
inside each game.

A game theme changes colours, decoration, background, typeface and
transition. Holding card, logos, timer and sounds stay the same for every
game. With a single game this feature is not there: the main theme always
applies.

## 7. Save your work

When you are done press **Save**, at the top.

On Chrome and Edge it asks once where to put the file, and from then on every
Save rewrites that one. On Firefox and Safari it downloads a new file each
time: take the newest download and replace the old file with it.

**Save before closing.** Your work lives inside that file.

The **More** menu, next to Save, has:

- **Download a copy**: an extra file, for instance to keep as a backup
- **Load from a file**: brings in the setup from another saved file
- **Step-by-step instructions**: this guide

## 8. On the night

1. Double-click the file
2. Press **Open external screen**, drag the window to the projector, press **F**
3. In the **Games** tab find the round and press the little triangle **▶**
4. The big screen shows the clock stopped, with the team name (if the round
   has one)
5. When the team is ready, press the **space bar**: the clock starts

From there you drive it with the keyboard, or with the buttons in the right
column: each one shows its key.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Space** | Starts and stops the clock |
| **Right arrow** | Moves on one item |
| **Left arrow** | Goes back |
| **R** | Resets the clock |
| **+** and **−** | Add or remove 5 seconds *while the round runs* |
| **I** | Back to the first item |
| **T** | Puts the holding card on the big screen |
| **X** | Ends the round |

Prefer other keys? Change them in the **Keys** tab: click a key and press the
new one.

The keys work from both the control window and the big one, but only while a
round is running.

> **Pressed the wrong thing?** Use **+** and **−** to fix the clock, or type
> the right value in the *Adjust the time* box and press *Set*. The round does
> not restart.

When time runs out the sound plays and the screen goes back to the holding
card.

## 9. If something goes wrong

**The second window will not open.** The browser blocked it. Look for the
warning icon in the address bar and allow pop-ups for this page.

**The computer screen goes dark mid-round.** It should not, but if it does,
open your power settings and raise the sleep timeout.

**I lost my changes.** Reopen the file: if the browser holds a newer version
than the saved one it tells you and offers to restore it.

**I messed up the look.** Use the **↺** arrows next to the settings, or **Back
to the last save** at the top of the Appearance panel.

**I downloaded a new version of the app and lost everything.** Nothing is lost:
open the new version, open **More** at the top, press **Load from a file** and
pick your old file. Your setup transfers across: features added since then
start switched off, everything else is as you left it. Then press **Save**.
